Tether Install guide

Install Tether on your Mac.

About two minutes, once. After this, Tether opens like any other app.

Why macOS shows a warning.

Tether isn't verified by Apple yet, so the first time you open it macOS says: Apple could not verify “Tether” is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy. That's the standard message for any app Apple hasn't checked yet. It doesn't mean something is wrong with Tether.

We're getting Tether verified (notarized) by Apple and will update the app as soon as possible. After that update, you won't need any of the steps below.

  1. 1Install. Put Tether in Applications.

    1. Download Tether.dmg and open it.
    2. Drag Tether onto the Applications folder.
    3. Eject the Tether disk in Finder (optional).
    Download Tether.dmg macOS 13 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

  2. 2First open. Allow it once in System Settings.

    1. Open Applications and double-click Tether. When the warning appears, click Done. Don't click Move to Trash.
    2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down to the Security section.
    3. Next to the message “Tether” was blocked to protect your Mac, click Open Anyway and enter your Mac password (or use Touch ID).
    4. Open Tether again and click Open. From now on it opens normally.

    macOS 15 or later: right-click → Open no longer skips this warning. It has to be done in System Settings as above. The Open Anyway button shows up for about an hour after the warning; if you don't see it, open Tether once more and check again.

  3. 3Screen Recording. Needed to show your screen on the iPhone.

    1. In Tether, add the iPhone screen and click Share screen. macOS asks to allow screen recording.
    2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording.
    3. Turn on Tether. If macOS asks, click Quit & Reopen.

    If the iPhone stays black, or Tether says sharing didn't start, this switch is usually off. Turn it on, then quit and reopen Tether.

    After updating Tether: macOS may treat the new version as a different app, so sharing stops working even though Tether still looks switched on. In Screen & System Audio Recording, select Tether, remove it with −, add it again with + (from Applications), then quit and reopen Tether. This goes away once Tether is verified by Apple.

  4. 4Optional: Accessibility. Only for clicking and typing from the iPhone.

    You only need this if you turn on More options → Let the iPhone click and type too. Using the Mac's own mouse and keyboard on the iPhone screen needs nothing extra.

    1. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
    2. Turn on Tether (click + and choose it from Applications if it isn't listed).

Something not working?

No Open Anyway button in Privacy & Security.
Double-click Tether once more so the warning appears again, then go straight back to System Settings. The button only shows up shortly after a blocked attempt.
macOS says Tether is “damaged”.
Delete it, download Tether.dmg again, and drag it to Applications again. If it still happens and you're comfortable with Terminal, this removes the download flag for Tether only: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Tether.app
The iPhone can't open the link or code.
Put the Mac and the iPhone on the same Wi-Fi, then scan the QR code shown in Tether.
